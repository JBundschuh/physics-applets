Model concept

Discrete 1D heat conduction model:

Tᵢnew = Tᵢ + a · (Tᵢ₋₁ − 2Tᵢ + Tᵢ₊₁)

The grid nodes move according to their temperature. Their motion illustrates thermal agitation but does not cause heat conduction.
