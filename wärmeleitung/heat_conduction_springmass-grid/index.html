Model concept

Model temperature depends on the kinetic energy of each node:

Tmodel ∼ vx2 + vy2

Springs couple neighboring nodes. Random changes in velocity direction make the motion less ordered without changing its kinetic energy.

Model limitation: This is not an exact atomic simulation. Each node represents a simplified region of a solid.
