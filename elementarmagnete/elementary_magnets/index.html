Why do neighboring magnetic moments align?

The quantum-mechanical exchange interaction favors parallel alignment of neighboring magnetic moments. The applet represents its effects using a simplified classical model.

Model assumptions and limitations

Model: The sample is a two-dimensional grid of representative magnetic moments. Exchange, anisotropy, dipolar interactions, and the external field are included. One arrow does not represent an entire Weiss domain.

Temperature and time: The moments undergo relaxation and thermal fluctuations. Temperature and time are dimensionless model quantities; actual Curie temperatures and relaxation times are not calculated.

Approximations: The external magnet is approximated by multiple dipole sources. Remanence can be explored qualitatively; a quantitative hysteresis curve is not calculated.
